# My agent: FitCoach AI
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps fitness enthusiasts plan workouts, track progress, and learn proper exercise technique with a catalog of exercises and personalized training routines.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: User fitness goals (hypertrophy, endurance, strength), experience level, available equipment, injury history/limitations, and personal records
- Tools: Exercise library lookup by muscle group/equipment, workout logging, rest/recovery recommendations
- Catalog/UI: Exercise cards with targeted muscle groups and difficulty, weekly workout split tables, routine breakdown cards
- Image gen: Form visual cues, anatomical muscle activation illustrations, celebration badges for hitting new personal records
- Sandbox: 1-rep max (1RM) estimation, training volume/progressive overload calculations, target heart rate zone computations

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): Code sandbox for 1RM and progressive overload calculations, wearable health data simulation
